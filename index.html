How to

Draw: click a grid point to start, move the mouse, click to place each bend. The line snaps to the nearest pipe direction, or to the one you lock with the buttons / keys NSEWUD (A = auto). Type a number to set the length exactly, Enter to place it. Esc or double-click ends the run. Click any existing bend, or any grid point along a straight leg, to start a branch from it (it becomes a tee in 3D); click the last point of a run to keep going.

Weld: click on a line to add a weld (it snaps to the elbow if you click near a bend). Drag a balloon anywhere to move it (any tool); its leader stays on the weld. Click a balloon to remove the weld, Shift-click to flip it back to the other side. Numbers follow the flow automatically.

Cross: where two lines cross, click the line that should be BEHIND, just off the crossing. It gets a small gap so the other line reads as in front (the line under your mouse turns orange first). Click the gap again to remove it.

Ends: click the end of a run and pick: Wave (iso stops, line continues), Wave + EXISTING, EOL (cap) or Plain.

Pan: right-drag or Space-drag. Zoom: scroll. Undo: Ctrl+Z.
